Project Description

What is the topic of your project?

Creating a bookstore that will offer a wide variety of books

Why did you choose this topic?

Creating a bookstore website is my dream, and I've always wanted to have a place where I can store all the books I have. Additionally, many of my friends have expressed difficulties in organizing their PDF or online books.

I have always been interested in creating a website or app that features a diverse selection of books.

What background knowledge to you bring to this topic?

I have always been interested in creating a website or app that combines a large section of books.

Wh4t is your intended audience?

The intended audience for this project is anyone who enjoys reading, especially students.
